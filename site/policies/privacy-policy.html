Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ELDT Training ("ELDT Training," "we," "us") collects, uses, and discloses personal information when you visit eldttraining.us, enroll in a course, or contact us.

Information we collect

Information you provide. When you enroll or contact us, we collect your name, email address, phone number, billing address, company name (optional), and the course you purchase. Payment card details are entered into a secure form provided by our payment processor, Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.

Training records. To deliver your course and meet federal requirements, our learning platform records your course progress and assessment results. When you complete an ELDT course, we submit the information required by 49 CFR 380.717 (such as your name, driver's license number and state, training type, and completion date) to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry.

Information collected automatically. When you browse our site, we and our service providers collect device and usage information such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referring pages, and interactions, using cookies and similar technologies.

How we use information

  • To process enrollments and payments and create your training account
  • To deliver courses, provide support, and send service messages and receipts
  • To report training completions to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry as required by law
  • To understand how our site is used and improve it
  • To measure and improve our advertising
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations

How we share information

  • Training partner: Courses are delivered on a learning platform operated by our partner, HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC, which processes enrollment and training records on our behalf.
  • Payment processing: Stripe processes payments.
  • Regulators: We submit required training information to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry, where it is available to state driver licensing agencies.
  • Analytics and advertising: We use Google Analytics, Google Ads, and Microsoft Clarity to understand site usage and measure advertising. These providers may collect information through cookies and similar technologies. When you complete a purchase, we may share hashed contact information with Google to measure conversions (enhanced conversions).
  • Legal and safety: We may disclose information to comply with law or protect rights, property, and safety.

We do not sell personal information for money. Some sharing for targeted advertising may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" under certain U.S. state laws; see Your Privacy Choices.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising. To make a request, email info@eldttraining.us or call (866) 429-6742. Some training records must be retained to meet federal requirements. We honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out of targeted advertising for the browser that sends them.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings, opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on, and manage Google ad personalization in your Google account settings.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information. We keep information as long as needed to provide our services, meet legal and regulatory obligations (including training record requirements), resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Children

Our courses are intended for adults seeking commercial driver credentials and are not directed to children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the updated version here with a new "last updated" date.
