ELDT Training in Iowa: Online CDL Theory Courses

Complete your federally required Entry-Level Driver Training theory online and get ready for your Iowa CDL skills test. Our courses meet FMCSA requirements and are reported to the Training Provider Registry that Iowa Department of Transportation, Motor Vehicle Division checks before testing.

Quick answer

Yes, you can complete ELDT theory online in Iowa. If you are getting a Class A or Class B CDL for the first time, upgrading from B to A, or adding a first-time H, P, or S endorsement, you must finish ELDT with an FMCSA-registered provider before Iowa Department of Transportation, Motor Vehicle Division will give you the skills test (or the H knowledge test). Theory can be done online; Class A, B, P, and S also need behind-the-wheel training.

How to get your CDL in Iowa with ELDT

  1. Meet the basic requirements. You need a valid Iowa driver's license, proof of identity and residency, and a DOT medical examiner's certificate from a provider on the FMCSA National Registry. Federal rules require you to be 21 to drive across state lines; most states allow intrastate-only CDLs from age 18.
  2. Get your commercial learner's permit (CLP). Pass the general knowledge test (plus any endorsement knowledge tests) with Iowa Department of Transportation, Motor Vehicle Division. You must hold the CLP for at least 14 days before the skills test.
  3. Complete ELDT theory online. Take the Class A or Class B course (and any endorsement courses), score 80% or higher, and we report it to the Training Provider Registry. You can do this before or after getting your CLP.
  4. Complete behind-the-wheel training. Train on a range and on public roads with a TPR-registered BTW provider such as a CDL school or a carrier's training program.
  5. Take the CDL skills test. Iowa Department of Transportation, Motor Vehicle Division confirms your ELDT records in the TPR, then you take the vehicle inspection, basic control, and road test.

Commercial driving in Iowa

Grain, livestock, ethanol, and I-80 coast-to-coast freight make Iowa a strong market for agricultural and over-the-road drivers. Major freight routes include I-80, I-35, I-29, I-380, and the largest CDL job markets are in and around Des Moines, Cedar Rapids, Davenport and Sioux City.

Whether you plan to drive regionally inside Iowa or run interstate routes, the federal ELDT requirement is the same: finish theory and (where required) behind-the-wheel training with a registered provider before your test.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take ELDT theory training online in Iowa?

Yes. ELDT is a federal requirement under 49 CFR Part 380, and theory training may be completed online with any provider listed on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry. Iowa Department of Transportation, Motor Vehicle Division checks the same national registry as every other state, so online theory from ELDT Training counts for a Iowa CDL.

Does Iowa Department of Transportation, Motor Vehicle Division verify my ELDT before the skills test?

Yes. Before administering the Class A or Class B skills test, the P or S skills test, or the H knowledge test, Iowa Department of Transportation, Motor Vehicle Division must confirm through the Training Provider Registry that you completed the required ELDT.

Do I need behind-the-wheel training in Iowa?

For Class A, Class B, Passenger (P), and School Bus (S), yes. Behind-the-wheel training must be completed with a TPR-registered provider in a representative vehicle. The Hazmat (H) endorsement is theory only.

Can I do behind-the-wheel training outside Iowa?

Federal rules allow you to train with any registered BTW provider, but a provider giving in-person training must meet the rules of the state where the training takes place, and your home state may add requirements. Confirm with Iowa Department of Transportation, Motor Vehicle Division before you book out-of-state BTW.

How quickly will my completion show up?

Training providers must submit your completion to the TPR by midnight of the second business day after you finish. We submit as soon as you pass the final assessment.
