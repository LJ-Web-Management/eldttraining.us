Frequently asked questions

Is ELDT different in each state?

The core ELDT rules are federal and identical everywhere: the same curricula, the same Training Provider Registry, and the same test-verification step. States can add requirements, especially for behind-the-wheel training delivered within their borders, so always check with your licensing agency.

Can I use an online ELDT provider based in another state?

Yes. Online theory training is not tied to a state. What matters is that the provider is listed on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry and reports your completion there.
