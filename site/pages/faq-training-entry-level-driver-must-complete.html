All ELDT questions

What is ELDT?

ELDT (Entry-Level Driver Training) is a set of federal training requirements established by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) in 49 CFR Part 380 for individuals seeking a commercial driver's license or certain CDL endorsements for the first time. Read the full guide: What is ELDT?

Who needs to complete ELDT?

Anyone getting a Class A or Class B CDL for the first time, upgrading from Class B to Class A, or getting a Passenger (P), School Bus (S), or Hazardous Materials (H) endorsement for the first time.

When did the ELDT requirements take effect?

February 7, 2022. They are not retroactive to individuals who already held the applicable CDL or endorsement before that date.

Can I complete ELDT training online?

Yes, the theory portion, as long as the provider is on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry. Behind-the-wheel training for Class A, Class B, P, and S must be completed in a vehicle with a qualified instructor.

What score do I need on the ELDT theory test?

At least 80% overall on the theory assessment.

How many hours is ELDT?

There's no federal minimum. Our courses run about 15 hours (Class A), 13 (Class B), 12 (B-to-A), 6 (Passenger), and 5 (Hazmat, School Bus, X). See how long ELDT takes.

Do I need my CDL permit before starting ELDT theory?

No. FMCSA doesn't require a CLP before theory training. You need a CLP for behind-the-wheel training on public roads and must hold it 14 days before the skills test.

Do I need behind-the-wheel training for the hazmat endorsement?

No. The H endorsement requires theory only.

Can I use different providers for theory and behind-the-wheel?

Yes. Both must be listed on the Training Provider Registry, and each reports its own portion.

How quickly is my training reported?

Providers must report by midnight of the second business day after completion. We submit as soon as you pass.

How does my state know I completed ELDT?

Your state driver licensing agency checks the FMCSA Training Provider Registry before the skills test (or the H knowledge test). You don't need to bring a paper certificate, though you can download one from us.

Does ELDT expire?

FMCSA's Training Provider Registry FAQs don't set an expiration date for ELDT certification. Your commercial learner's permit does expire, so plan to test while it's valid.

Do I need ELDT for a Tanker (N) or Doubles/Triples (T) endorsement?

No. Federal ELDT covers Class A, Class B, H, P, and S only. Our Tanker (N) course is optional training to help you pass the N knowledge test.

Do I need ELDT to upgrade from Class B to Class A?

Yes. A B-to-A upgrade is specifically covered by the ELDT rule. See our Class B to A upgrade course.

I already have my learner's permit. Is this course another permit test?

No. If you have your CLP, you've passed your state's written permit tests. ELDT is the federally required training that must be completed and submitted to the Training Provider Registry before your CDL skills test. The course quizzes and final exam are for training completion only and are separate from the DMV tests.

Is ELDT required in every state?

Yes. It's a federal requirement. Online theory from a registered provider is accepted nationwide. See ELDT by state.

Are veterans exempt from ELDT?

Military drivers who qualify for the skills-test waiver under 49 CFR 383.77 aren't subject to ELDT for that license class. See ELDT exemptions.

How much does ELDT cost?

Our online theory courses range from $49.99 to $129.99. Class A is $129.99. See ELDT cost.

Is the course in Spanish?

No. Our courses are English only, with audio playback that reads each lesson aloud.

Can my employer pay for my course?

Yes. Employers can purchase courses for drivers. Call (866) 429-6742.

What's your refund policy?

You can cancel for a full refund within 30 days of purchase, unless 65% or more of the course has been completed or a certificate has been issued. See the full refund policy.
