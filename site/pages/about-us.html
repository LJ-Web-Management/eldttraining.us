About ELDT Training

Our mission is to empower aspiring commercial drivers with the knowledge they need to succeed on the road, starting with federally compliant, affordable ELDT theory training.

Our goal

At ELDTTraining.us, our mission is to empower aspiring commercial drivers with the skills and knowledge needed to succeed on the road. We specialize in Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT), designed to meet FMCSA standards while delivering an engaging and comprehensive learning experience.

Empowering future drivers with expert training

Our approach combines industry expertise with modern learning tools, including interactive videos, quizzes, animations, audio narration, and real-world scenarios. Students don't just meet compliance requirements; they build the confidence and responsibility needed to excel as commercial drivers.

Whether you're just starting your career in commercial driving or you're an employer looking for a reliable training partner, ELDTTraining.us is committed to helping you reach your goals with expert guidance, a user-friendly platform, and a focus on safety.

How our courses are built

Every course follows the federal curriculum for its training type in the appendices to 49 CFR Part 380. Lessons are developed by instructors with decades of combined commercial driving and safety training experience, reviewed against current FMCSA regulations and guidance, and delivered through our learning platform with quizzes, a practice exam, and a final theory assessment requiring 80% or higher.

Who we are

ELDTtraining.us is a subsidiary of Industrial Certified Training, LLC, a company specializing in DOT safety training. We partner with HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC to deliver a comprehensive range of safety compliance courses, including the learning platform our students use.

Eldttraining.us is a private company. Our courses align with FMCSA and DOT guidelines, and we are listed as a registered training provider on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by FMCSA, USDOT, or any state licensing agency.

Our editorial standards

Our ELDT and CDL guides are written and reviewed by our curriculum team. We cite primary sources such as the Electronic Code of Federal Regulations and the FMCSA Training Provider Registry, date every update, and correct errors promptly. Spot something out of date? Email info@eldttraining.us.
