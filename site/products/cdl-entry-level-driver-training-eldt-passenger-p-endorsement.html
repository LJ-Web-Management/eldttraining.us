Passenger (P) Endorsement ELDT Course Online

A Passenger (P) endorsement lets you drive a commercial vehicle designed to carry 16 or more people, including the driver. If you are getting the P endorsement for the first time, the federal ELDT rule requires P endorsement theory training from a TPR-registered provider, plus behind-the-wheel training, before your P skills test.

This course covers the passenger curriculum in Appendix C to 49 CFR Part 380: vehicle orientation and inspections, post-crash and emergency procedures, passenger management and baggage, hours of service and seat-belt rules, navigating railroad crossings and highways, and security and roadside inspections. Pass the final assessment with 80% or higher and we report your completion to the TPR.

Who needs this course?

  • Transit, city bus, and motorcoach drivers
  • Shuttle and tour drivers operating vehicles designed for 16+ occupants
  • School bus drivers (who also need the School Bus endorsement)
  • Church, camp, and nonprofit drivers of large passenger vans and buses

You do not need this course if: Drivers who held a P endorsement before February 7, 2022.

Frequently asked questions

Do school bus drivers need the P endorsement ELDT?

Yes. To drive a school bus you need both the P and S endorsements on a Class A, B, or C CDL. Each endorsement requires its own ELDT theory course and behind-the-wheel training before its skills test.

Can I get a P endorsement on a Class C CDL?

Yes. A Class C CDL with a P endorsement covers smaller vehicles designed to carry 16 or more people that are under 26,001 lbs GVWR. P endorsement ELDT applies to Class C applicants too.

How long is the P endorsement course?

About 6 hours across 18 lessons, self-paced, with an 80% passing score on the final assessment.

I already have my learner’s permit in hand. Do I need to take more tests in this course, or is it just a precursor to take the test I have already passed?

If you already have your CDL learner’s permit in hand, then you have already passed the written permit tests required by your state. This ELDT course is not another DMV permit test. It is the federally required Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) theory training that must be completed and submitted to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry before you can take your CDL behind-the-wheel skills test (pre-trip, backing, and road test). You will complete quizzes and a final exam within the course itself, but those are for training completion purposes only and are separate from the DMV permit exams you already passed.
