Hazmat Endorsement: How to Get Your CDL H Endorsement

The H endorsement has three parts: training, a test, and a federal security check. Here's how to get through all three.

Updated November 16, 20264 min readReviewed by the ELDT Training curriculum team
Quick answer

To get a hazmat (H) endorsement: (1) complete hazmat ELDT theory with an FMCSA-registered provider, (2) pass your state's hazmat knowledge test, and (3) pass the TSA security threat assessment (online application, fingerprints, and fee). You must hold or be getting a CDL, and in practice be at least 21. Hazmat ELDT is theory only, with no behind-the-wheel requirement, so it can be done entirely online. Most drivers get the endorsement within a few weeks, depending on TSA processing.

Who needs a hazmat endorsement?

You need an H endorsement to drive a CMV transporting hazardous materials in a quantity that requires placards under the Hazardous Materials Regulations, or any quantity of certain select agents and toxins. Common hazmat loads include fuel, compressed gases (propane), corrosives, explosives, flammable solids, and radioactive materials.

How much hazmat can you carry without an endorsement? If the load doesn't require placards, you don't need the H endorsement. Placards are required for any amount of "Table 1" materials (for example, certain explosives and poison gases) and for 1,001 lbs or more aggregate gross weight of "Table 2" materials. Shippers and carriers determine placarding; when in doubt, don't drive a placarded load without the endorsement.

Step 1: Complete hazmat ELDT

If you're getting the H endorsement for the first time, you must complete hazmat theory training from a provider on the Training Provider Registry before the knowledge test. The curriculum (Appendix E to Part 380) covers:

  • Hazmat basics: the hazardous materials table, shipping papers, marking, labeling, placarding
  • Reporting crashes and releases
  • Tunnels and railroad crossings
  • Loading, unloading, segregation, and securement
  • Bulk packages, cargo tanks, portable tanks
  • Emergency equipment and emergency response
  • Fueling rules and tire checks
  • Route planning and hazardous materials safety permits

Our hazmat ELDT course covers all of it in 13 lessons (about 5 hours) for $69.99. Pass with 80% or higher and we report it to the TPR.

Step 2: Pass the hazmat knowledge test

After your provider reports your training, your state licensing agency can give you the H knowledge test. It's typically around 30 multiple-choice questions with an 80% passing score (exact format varies by state). Many states require you to retake it when you renew.

Step 3: Pass the TSA security threat assessment

The Transportation Security Administration's Hazardous Materials Endorsement Threat Assessment Program (HTAPP) is a background check:

  1. Apply online through TSA's enrollment provider.
  2. Visit an enrollment center for fingerprints and identity documents.
  3. Pay the fee (under $100; check TSA for the current amount).
  4. Wait for a determination. It can take several weeks, so start early. Your state is notified when you're approved.

You must be a U.S. citizen, lawful permanent resident, or meet other TSA immigration criteria, and not have certain disqualifying criminal offenses. Approval is generally valid for five years.

Order of steps

States differ on whether they want the TSA assessment started before or after the knowledge test. Many drivers start TSA paperwork as soon as they finish ELDT so the background check runs while they study.

Renewing your hazmat endorsement

Renewal doesn't require ELDT. You'll need a new TSA threat assessment (approval is good for up to five years) and, in many states, to pass the hazmat knowledge test again. Start the TSA renewal at least 60 days before your endorsement expires.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get a hazmat endorsement?

The ELDT course takes about 5 hours. The longest part is usually TSA processing, which can take several weeks. Many drivers finish within a month.

Do I need behind-the-wheel training for hazmat?

No. Hazmat ELDT is theory only. There's no hazmat skills test.

Where can I get fingerprinted for the hazmat endorsement?

At a TSA enrollment center operated by TSA's enrollment provider. You schedule the appointment when you apply online.

Can I get a hazmat endorsement with a CDL permit?

No. Hazmat can't be added to a CLP. You need a CDL to hold the H endorsement.

Do I need hazmat ELDT if I had an H endorsement before?

If you held the H endorsement before February 7, 2022, you don't need ELDT to renew it or to get it back.

Is the hazmat endorsement worth it?

For many drivers, yes. It opens fuel, chemical, and specialized freight jobs that often pay more.
