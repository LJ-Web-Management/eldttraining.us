The ELDT Test: Theory Assessment, Passing Score, and Practice Questions

The ELDT theory assessment explained, plus a free practice quiz built from the federal curriculum.

Updated November 16, 20264 min readReviewed by the ELDT Training curriculum team
Quick answer

The ELDT test is the theory assessment at the end of your ELDT course. You must score at least 80% overall to pass. It's given by your training provider (written or electronic), not by the DMV, and covers the federal curriculum for your license or endorsement. When you pass, the provider reports your completion to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry. It's separate from your state's CDL permit tests.

If you already have your CLP, you've passed the state tests. The ELDT assessment is the additional federally required training check.

What happens if you fail the ELDT test?

Federal rules require a minimum score of 80% but don't limit retakes. Most providers, including ours, let you review the material and try again. Ask your provider about its retake policy before you enroll.

How to prepare for the ELDT assessment

  • Complete every lesson and quiz; don't rush the non-driving units (hours of service, drugs and alcohol, whistleblower rules).
  • Use audio playback to review while commuting.
  • Retake practice quizzes until you're comfortably above 80%.
  • Review the numbers: hours-of-service limits, BAC limits, warning device placement, tire tread depths, railroad crossing distances.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free ELDT practice test?

The 12 questions above are a free sample. Our courses include quizzes after lessons and a practice exam before the final assessment.

Is the ELDT test online?

For online theory courses, yes, the assessment is taken online at the end of the course.

Are ELDT test answers available as a PDF?

Legitimate providers don't publish their assessment answers. Study the curriculum instead; the assessment checks that you learned it.

How many questions are on the ELDT test?

FMCSA doesn't set a number. Each provider designs its assessment to cover the full curriculum, and you need 80% overall.
