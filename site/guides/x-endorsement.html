What Is the X Endorsement on a CDL?

X is the one code that says you can haul hazmat in a tank. Here's how to earn it.

Updated November 16, 20262 min readReviewed by the ELDT Training curriculum team
Quick answer

The X endorsement is a combined Hazardous Materials (H) and Tank Vehicle (N) endorsement. It lets you haul placarded hazardous materials in tank vehicles, such as gasoline, diesel, and liquid chemicals. To get it, you pass both the hazmat and tanker knowledge tests and the TSA security threat assessment. If you don't already have an H endorsement, you must complete hazmat ELDT theory before the hazmat test.

What the X endorsement lets you do

  • Haul placarded hazmat in tank vehicles (fuel, liquid chemicals, compressed gases)
  • Haul non-hazmat liquids in tanks (the N privileges)
  • Haul placarded hazmat in non-tank vehicles (the H privileges)

States print X instead of separate H and N codes when you qualify for both.

How to get the X endorsement

  1. Complete hazmat ELDT (or our X course, which includes hazmat theory plus tank vehicle content).
  2. Start your TSA threat assessment online and schedule fingerprints.
  3. Pass the hazmat and tanker knowledge tests at your state licensing agency.
  4. Receive your X endorsement once TSA approval reaches your state.

Frequently asked questions

Is the X endorsement the same as hazmat?

No. X includes hazmat plus tanker. A driver with only H can haul placarded hazmat in non-tank vehicles but not in tank vehicles.

Does the X endorsement require ELDT?

The hazmat part does, if you're getting hazmat for the first time. The federal ELDT rule doesn't cover the tanker part.

How much does an X endorsement cost?

Our X course is $129.99. Add the TSA fee (under $100) and your state's test and endorsement fees.

Is the X endorsement worth it?

For fuel and chemical hauling, yes; it's required. These jobs often pay more than general freight.
