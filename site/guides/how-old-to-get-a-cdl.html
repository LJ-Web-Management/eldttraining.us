Getting Your CDL

How Old Do You Have to Be to Get a CDL?

The short version: 18 to drive within your state, 21 to cross state lines. Here are the details and exceptions.

Updated November 16, 20263 min readReviewed by the ELDT Training curriculum team
Quick answer

You must be at least 18 to get a CDL in most states, but drivers under 21 are limited to intrastate driving (within one state). Federal rules (49 CFR 391.11) require you to be 21 to drive a commercial vehicle in interstate commerce, and in practice 21 to haul placarded hazmat. ELDT rules apply the same way at any age: if you're getting your first Class A or B CDL, you need ELDT before your skills test.

CDL age rules at a glance

AgeWhat you can do
Under 18No CDL
18 to 20CLP and CDL in most states, intrastate only (often shown as a K restriction)
21+Interstate driving, most hazmat work, full CDL privileges

Why 18-year-olds are limited to intrastate

The federal driver qualification rules require interstate commercial drivers to be at least 21. States can license 18- to 20-year-olds for intrastate commerce, so a young driver can haul freight between cities in the same state but not across state lines. Federal pilot programs for under-21 interstate drivers have been very limited, so plan on intrastate work until you turn 21.

What jobs can you get with a CDL under 21?

  • Local and regional delivery within your state
  • Construction, dump truck, and concrete work
  • Agricultural hauling
  • Municipal and utility work
  • Some intrastate company driver jobs at larger carriers

Many large interstate carriers hire at 21 and up, and some require 22 or 23 for insurance reasons.

Age and endorsements

  • Hazmat (H): most states and the hazmat transportation rules effectively require you to be 21.
  • Passenger and School Bus: many states and employers set minimum ages of 21 or older for school bus drivers.
  • Tanker and Doubles/Triples: follow the general CDL age rules, but employers often require experience.

Do younger drivers need ELDT?

Yes. ELDT is based on the license you're getting, not your age. An 18-year-old getting a first-time Class A CDL needs Class A ELDT theory and BTW, exactly like a 40-year-old.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a CDL at 17?

No. The minimum age for a CLP or CDL is 18 in every state.

Can I start ELDT before I turn 18?

FMCSA doesn't set an age for theory training, but you can't get a CLP or take the skills test until you meet your state's age requirement. Check with your provider and state.

Is there a maximum age for a CDL?

No. As long as you meet the medical and other requirements, there's no upper age limit.

Does the intrastate restriction come off automatically at 21?

It depends on the state. Many require you to visit and request removal of the K restriction once you turn 21 and have a valid medical certificate.
