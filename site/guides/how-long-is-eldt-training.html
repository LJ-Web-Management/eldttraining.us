How Long Is ELDT Training, and How Long Are Results Valid?

Typical course lengths, why there's no required minimum, how fast your completion reaches your state, and what actually expires.

Updated November 16, 20264 min readReviewed by the ELDT Training curriculum team
Quick answer

There is no federal minimum number of hours for ELDT. Online theory courses are built to take roughly 5 to 15 hours depending on the license or endorsement: about 15 hours for Class A, 13 for Class B, 12 for the B-to-A upgrade, and 5 to 6 for H, P, or S. You finish when you score 80% or higher on the final assessment. Your provider must report completion to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry within two business days. FMCSA's FAQs set no expiration date for ELDT records, but your commercial learner's permit does expire.

These are course design estimates. Because ELDT is proficiency-based, you can move faster through material you know and slower through new topics. Most people spread Class A theory over several days or a couple of weeks while working.

How long does behind-the-wheel training take?

BTW also has no minimum hours. Your instructor decides when you've shown proficiency in each skill. Depending on experience and the program, Class A BTW often takes anywhere from a few days to a few weeks. Full-time CDL school programs (theory + BTW) commonly run 3 to 7 weeks.

How fast does my completion reach the FMCSA?

Under 49 CFR 380.717, your training provider must submit your training certification to the Training Provider Registry by midnight of the second business day after you complete the training. At ELDT Training, we submit as soon as you pass the final assessment. Once submitted, your state driver licensing agency can see the record when it checks before your test.

How long are ELDT theory results valid?

This is one of the most-searched ELDT questions. Here's what the rules and FMCSA guidance actually say:

  • FMCSA's Training Provider Registry FAQs do not list an expiration date for ELDT theory or BTW certifications.
  • FMCSA does not require theory and BTW to happen in a particular order. BTW just has to be complete before the skills test.
  • Your commercial learner's permit does expire. A CLP is valid for no more than one year under federal rules (states may issue shorter permits with renewal). Plan theory, BTW, and testing within your CLP's validity.
  • Your provider must be active on the TPR when you train. Training delivered after a provider is removed from the registry is invalid. Training you completed while the provider was listed is a separate question; if your provider is removed, contact your state licensing agency.
  • Some states and employers set their own time limits. If you plan to wait a long time between theory and testing, confirm with your state licensing agency.

Practical advice

Finish theory first (it's online and quick), then schedule BTW and your skills test while your CLP is valid. Our courses give you a 3-month window to complete the course and 1 year of access to review the material before your tests.

How long does the whole CDL process take?

With focused effort, many drivers go from zero to CDL in about 4 to 8 weeks: medical exam and CLP (days), 14-day minimum CLP holding period, online theory (days to two weeks), BTW (days to weeks), then the skills test. See how long it takes to get a CDL.

Frequently asked questions

Can I finish ELDT theory in one day?

For short courses like hazmat (about 5 hours), yes. Class A theory is about 15 hours of material, so most people take several sessions. There's no federal minimum, so the pace is up to you as long as you pass with 80% or higher.

How long do I have to finish the online course?

Our courses include a 3-month completion window and 1 year of access to the training materials.

Does ELDT need to be renewed?

No. ELDT is a one-time requirement for each license class or endorsement you obtain for the first time. There is no federal ELDT renewal or refresher.

What if my ELDT doesn't show up after two business days?

Contact your training provider first; only providers can submit records. If the provider confirms submission, ask your state licensing agency to re-check the TPR.

Does failing the skills test reset my ELDT?

No. Your training record stays in the registry. You retest under your state's retest rules.
