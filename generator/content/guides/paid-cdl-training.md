:::answer
**Paid (company-sponsored) CDL training means an employer covers your CDL training, including ELDT theory and behind-the-wheel, usually in exchange for agreeing to drive for them for a set period, often around a year.** Some programs pay you during training. If you leave early, you may have to repay part or all of the training cost. These programs must still use providers on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry, so your ELDT records are valid anywhere.
:::

## How company-sponsored training usually works

1. **Apply and get conditionally hired** (background, driving record, drug test, DOT physical).
2. **Get your CLP**, often with the company's help.
3. **Complete ELDT theory and BTW** through the company's registered school or a partner school.
4. **Pass the skills test**, often in the company's truck.
5. **Drive with a trainer** for a period of supervised over-the-road experience.
6. **Fulfill the work commitment** to have the training cost forgiven.

## Types of paid CDL training

| Type | How it's paid | Typical catch |
|---|---|---|
| Company-sponsored school | Carrier pays tuition | Work commitment; repayment if you leave |
| Tuition reimbursement | You pay, carrier reimburses over time | Must stay employed to get full reimbursement |
| Paid training (wages) | Carrier pays you while training | Often lower pay during training phase |
| Public employer programs | District, transit, or city pays | Usually local jobs, sometimes a commitment |

## Questions to ask before you sign

- How long is the work commitment, and how is the repayment amount calculated if I leave early?
- Is the repayment prorated?
- Are you a TPR-registered provider, or which registered school will train me?
- Am I paid during training? How much, and for which phases?
- What kind of runs will I do after training (OTR, regional, local), and how much home time?
- What happens if I fail a test?

## Frequently asked questions

### Which trucking companies pay for CDL training?
Many large truckload and LTL carriers, as well as some private fleets, run company-sponsored or tuition-reimbursement programs. Offerings change often, so check carriers' current recruiting pages and compare contracts.

### Is paid CDL training really free?
The upfront cost may be zero, but you usually owe a work commitment, and leaving early can mean repaying training costs.

### Is my ELDT from a company program valid with other employers?
Yes. ELDT records in the Training Provider Registry belong to your driver record, not the employer. Your CDL is yours.

### Can I get paid CDL training with housing?
Some programs provide lodging during training, especially when the school is far from home. Ask what's included.
