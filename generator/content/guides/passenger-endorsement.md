:::answer
**A Passenger (P) endorsement is required to drive a commercial vehicle designed to transport 16 or more passengers, including the driver.** To get it for the first time, you complete P endorsement ELDT (theory plus behind-the-wheel training) with FMCSA-registered providers, pass the passenger knowledge test, and pass a skills test in a passenger vehicle of the right class. School bus drivers need the P endorsement plus the S endorsement.
:::

## Who needs a passenger endorsement?

- City, transit, and paratransit bus drivers
- Motorcoach, charter, and tour bus drivers
- Airport, hotel, and casino shuttle drivers (vehicles designed for 16+)
- School bus drivers (with the S endorsement)
- Church, camp, and nonprofit drivers of large buses

The vehicle's **design capacity** matters, not how many people are actually on board.

## How to get a P endorsement

1. **Hold a CDL or CLP** of the right class (B for most full-size buses, C for smaller ones, A in some cases).
2. **Pass the passenger knowledge test** (it can be added to your CLP, but you can't carry passengers until fully endorsed).
3. **Complete P endorsement ELDT theory.** Our [Passenger (P) course](/products/cdl-entry-level-driver-training-eldt-passenger-p-endorsement) is 18 lessons, about 6 hours, for $59.99.
4. **Complete P endorsement BTW** with a registered provider in a passenger vehicle.
5. **Pass the skills test** in a passenger vehicle of the class you're licensed for.

## What P endorsement ELDT covers

Vehicle orientation, pre-trip/en route/post-trip inspection, fueling and idling, post-crash and emergency procedures, passenger management and baggage, hours of service, seat belt rules, railroad crossings, navigating highways and tunnels, security awareness, and roadside inspections.

## P endorsement on Class A, B, or C

| CDL class | Typical vehicles |
|---|---|
| Class B + P | Full-size transit buses, motorcoaches, many school buses |
| Class C + P | Smaller buses and passenger vans designed for 16+ under 26,001 lbs |
| Class A + P | Articulated buses or bus-plus-trailer combinations, plus everything below |

Testing in a smaller class of vehicle may add an M or N passenger restriction. See [CDL restrictions](/guides/cdl-restrictions).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need ELDT for a passenger endorsement?
Yes, if you're getting P for the first time and didn't hold it before February 7, 2022. You need both theory and behind-the-wheel training.

### Do I need a CDL to drive a 15-passenger van?
Generally no, if it's designed for 15 or fewer occupants including the driver. Vehicles designed for 16 or more require a CDL with P.

### How many questions are on the passenger endorsement test?
Typically around 20, with an 80% passing score. Format varies by state.

### Can I get the P endorsement on my permit?
Yes, P can appear on a CLP, but CLP holders with P may not carry passengers other than trainers, examiners, and other trainees.
