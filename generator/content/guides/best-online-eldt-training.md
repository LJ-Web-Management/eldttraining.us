:::answer
**The best online ELDT provider for you is one that is active on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry for your training type, reports completions quickly, is priced transparently, and fits how you learn** (mobile, audio, self-paced). Registration is non-negotiable: training from a provider that isn't listed, or that was removed, doesn't count. After that, compare price, course length, support, and refund policy.
:::

## The 8-point checklist

1. **Active on the TPR for your training type.** Search the [Training Provider Registry](/guides/training-provider-registry) and confirm the provider offers theory for Class A, Class B, P, S, or H, whichever you need.
2. **Clear, all-in price.** The price should include the final assessment and TPR reporting. Watch for add-on "filing fees."
3. **Fast reporting.** The legal deadline is midnight of the second business day. Better providers report as soon as you pass.
4. **Full curriculum.** The course should cover every unit in the federal curriculum, with a final assessment requiring 80% or higher.
5. **Fits your learning style.** Mobile-friendly design, audio narration, progress saving, and practice quizzes make a real difference.
6. **Reasonable completion window.** Look for enough time to finish and review before your tests.
7. **Real support.** A phone number, email, and posted hours, so you can get help with login or reporting questions.
8. **Fair refund policy.** Know what happens if you change your plans.

## Online theory vs a full CDL school

If you're starting from zero and want everything in one place, a CDL school that offers theory and BTW can be simpler. If you want to save money, keep working while you study, or already have a BTW provider lined up (such as an employer), online theory plus separate BTW is often the better value. Both paths are equally valid under federal rules as long as each provider is registered.

## Questions to ask any provider

- Which training types are you registered for on the TPR?
- When exactly do you submit my completion?
- What happens if I fail the final assessment?
- Can I access the course on my phone, and does it have audio?
- How long do I have to finish?
- What's your refund policy?

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the cheapest ELDT course good enough?
Any course from an active registered provider satisfies the federal requirement. The difference is in experience: learning quality, support, and reporting speed. Be cautious with extremely low prices from providers you can't verify on the TPR.

### Are FMCSA-approved ELDT courses better?
FMCSA doesn't approve or rank individual courses. Providers self-certify and are listed on the TPR, and FMCSA can audit and remove them. "TPR-registered" is the accurate term.

### Do employers prefer certain ELDT providers?
Most employers care that training is valid on the TPR and that you can pass your tests. Some carriers that run their own programs require you to train with them.

### Can I switch providers partway through?
Yes, but you'd generally have to complete the full theory course with the new provider, since each provider certifies its own complete training.
