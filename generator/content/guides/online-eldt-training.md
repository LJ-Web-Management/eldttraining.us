:::answer
**Yes. FMCSA allows ELDT theory training to be completed 100% online**, as long as the provider is listed on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry and the content is prepared by qualified theory instructors. Online theory is accepted in every state. You still need in-person behind-the-wheel training for Class A, Class B, Passenger (P), and School Bus (S). The Hazmat (H) endorsement is theory only, so it can be completed entirely online.
:::

## How online ELDT theory training works

1. **Enroll** in the course that matches your license or endorsement (Class A, Class B, B-to-A upgrade, H, P, or S).
2. **Get your login** by email and start right away. There is no class schedule.
3. **Work through the lessons** at your own pace on a phone, tablet, or computer. Good courses save your progress automatically and include audio narration.
4. **Take the quizzes and final assessment.** You need an overall score of **80% or higher**.
5. **The provider reports your completion** to the Training Provider Registry, within the federal deadline of midnight on the second business day.
6. **Your state sees it.** Your licensing agency checks the registry before your skills test (or H knowledge test).

## What you can and can't do online

| Training | Online? | Notes |

| Class B theory | Yes | Then BTW range + public road |
| Class B to A upgrade theory | Yes | Then Class A BTW |
| Hazmat (H) theory | Yes | No BTW, fully online |
| Passenger (P) theory | Yes | Then P BTW |
| School Bus (S) theory | Yes | Then S BTW |
| Behind-the-wheel (any) | No | In a real vehicle with a qualified instructor; simulators can't replace it |

## Is online ELDT legit?

Online theory is explicitly allowed by FMCSA. The question to ask is whether the **provider** is legitimate. A course only counts if the provider is listed on the TPR at the time you train. Since December 2025, FMCSA has removed thousands of non-compliant or inactive providers from the registry, and training conducted after a provider's removal date is invalid.

Before you pay, check that the provider:

- Appears on the [FMCSA Training Provider Registry](/guides/training-provider-registry) search, with **theory** listed for your training type
- Clearly states which training types it offers (Class A, Class B, P, S, H)
- Explains how and when it reports completions to the TPR
- Requires a final assessment with an 80% passing score (no "instant certificate" shortcuts)
- Lists real contact information and a refund policy

## Benefits of online ELDT theory

- **Lower cost.** Online theory usually costs a fraction of classroom programs. See [ELDT cost](/guides/eldt-cost).
- **Flexible schedule.** Study around your current job, at night, or on weekends.
- **Faster start.** Begin the same day you enroll; no waiting for a class to fill.
- **Self-paced.** Spend more time on topics that are new to you and move faster through what you already know.
- **Audio playback.** Listen to lessons, which helps if you learn better by hearing.

## Things to know before you buy

- **Language:** Many online courses, including ours, are English only. Federal rules also require CDL drivers to read and speak English well enough to do the job. See our guide to [English language proficiency for CDL drivers](/guides/cdl-english-language-proficiency).
- **Your name and license number matter.** The record sent to the TPR must match your driver's license or CLP, so enter your information exactly.
- **BTW is separate.** Line up a registered BTW provider (CDL school, community college, or carrier training program) for Class A, B, P, and S.
- **Completion windows:** Our courses give you 3 months to finish and 1 year of access to review materials.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is online ELDT accepted in my state?
Yes. ELDT is a federal requirement and every state checks the same national registry, so online theory from a TPR-registered provider is accepted in all 50 states and DC. Find your state on our [ELDT by state](/pages/state) page.

### How long does online ELDT take?
It depends on the course and your pace. Our Class A course is about 15 hours, Class B about 13 hours, and the hazmat course about 5 hours. There is no federal minimum.

### Can I do ELDT online for free?
Legitimate ELDT theory from a registered provider is rarely free unless an employer, workforce program, or school pays for it. See [free ELDT training options](/guides/free-eldt-training).

### Can I get my whole CDL online?
No. Theory can be online, but you must complete behind-the-wheel training in person (for Class A and B) and pass the in-person CDL skills test at a state-approved test site.

### Is online ELDT available in Spanish?
Some providers offer Spanish courses. Our courses are English only, with audio playback.
