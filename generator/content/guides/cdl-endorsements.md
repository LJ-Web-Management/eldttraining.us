:::answer
**There are six CDL endorsements: H (Hazardous Materials), N (Tank Vehicle), P (Passenger), S (School Bus), T (Doubles/Triples), and X (combined Hazmat and Tank Vehicle).** Each requires a knowledge test. P and S also require a skills test. Under the federal ELDT rule, first-time H, P, and S endorsements require Entry-Level Driver Training; N and T don't. Hazmat endorsements (H and X) also require a TSA security threat assessment.
:::

## Hazmat (H)
Required to haul hazardous materials in amounts that require placards. You need hazmat ELDT theory, the H knowledge test, and a TSA security threat assessment with fingerprints. Usually the highest-paying single endorsement. Full guide: [hazmat endorsement](/guides/hazmat-endorsement).

## Tank Vehicle (N)
Required for tanks with an individual capacity over 119 gallons and aggregate capacity of 1,000 gallons or more. Knowledge test only; ELDT isn't federally required. Full guide: [tanker endorsement](/guides/tanker-endorsement).

## Passenger (P)
Required for vehicles designed to carry 16 or more people including the driver: transit buses, motorcoaches, shuttles. Requires P ELDT (theory and BTW), a knowledge test, and a skills test. Full guide: [passenger endorsement](/guides/passenger-endorsement).

## School Bus (S)
Required to drive a school bus, in addition to P. Requires S ELDT (theory and BTW), a knowledge test, and a skills test in a school bus. Full guide: [school bus endorsement](/guides/school-bus-endorsement).

## Doubles/Triples (T)
Required to pull double or triple trailers with a Class A. Knowledge test only. Triples are legal only on certain routes in some states. Full guide: [doubles/triples endorsement](/guides/doubles-triples-endorsement).

## X (Hazmat + Tanker)
Issued when you qualify for both H and N. Covers placarded hazmat in tanks, like gasoline. Full guide: [X endorsement](/guides/x-endorsement).

## Which endorsements should you get?

- **Most job options as a Class A driver:** H, N (or X), and T
- **Fuel and chemical hauling:** X
- **Bus and transit:** P (Class B)
- **School bus:** P + S
- **Food-grade liquids, water, milk:** N

## Frequently asked questions

### How many CDL endorsements are there?
Six: H, N, P, S, T, and X. Some states also use additional state-specific codes.

### What endorsements are required for a Class A CDL?
None are required to get a Class A. You add endorsements based on the work you'll do. Many Class A drivers add H, N, and T to qualify for more jobs.

### Which CDL endorsements require ELDT?
First-time H, P, and S endorsements. X requires hazmat ELDT if you don't already have an H endorsement. N and T don't require ELDT.

### Can I get endorsements on my CDL permit?
Only P, S, and N can appear on a CLP, with restrictions (no passengers for P/S; empty tanks only for N). Hazmat can't be added to a CLP.

### Which endorsement pays the most?
Hazmat and tanker (or X) generally open the highest-paying specialized freight jobs.
