:::answer
**A tanker (N) endorsement is required to drive a CMV that carries liquid or gaseous materials in tanks with an individual rated capacity over 119 gallons and an aggregate capacity of 1,000 gallons or more** (49 CFR 383.5). To get it, you pass your state's tank vehicle knowledge test; there's no skills test and no federal ELDT requirement. If you'll haul hazmat in a tank, you need the X endorsement (or H plus N) instead.
:::

## What counts as a tank vehicle?

A tank vehicle is any CMV designed to transport liquid or gaseous materials in tanks that are permanently or temporarily attached to the vehicle or chassis, with:

- Any single tank rated **over 119 gallons**, and
- A combined (aggregate) rated capacity of **1,000 gallons or more**

Examples: fuel tankers, milk and food-grade tankers, water trucks, and flatbeds carrying large IBC totes that meet the capacity thresholds. Empty tanks still count; the endorsement depends on the vehicle's design capacity.

## How to get a tanker endorsement

1. Hold a CDL or CLP (N can be placed on a CLP, but CLP holders may only drive empty tanks).
2. Study the tank vehicle section of your state's CDL manual, or take a [tanker training course](/products/cdl-entry-level-driver-training-eldt-tanker-n-endorsement).
3. Pass the tank vehicle knowledge test (typically about 20 questions with an 80% passing score).
4. Pay your state's endorsement fee.

## Does the tanker endorsement require ELDT?

No. The federal ELDT rule covers Class A, Class B, and the H, P, and S endorsements. Tank vehicle training is still valuable, and many carriers require documented tanker training before you haul.

## What's on the tanker test

- **Liquid surge** and how it affects stopping and handling
- **Baffles and bulkheads** vs **smooth-bore tanks**
- **High center of gravity** and rollover risk on curves and ramps
- **Outage** (allowing room for liquid expansion)
- Inspection of valves, manhole covers, vents, and emergency shutoffs
- Braking and following distance with partial loads

## Frequently asked questions

### When do you need a tanker endorsement?
Whenever the vehicle's tanks meet the 119-gallon individual and 1,000-gallon aggregate thresholds, loaded or empty.

### How many questions are on the tanker endorsement test?
Typically around 20, with 80% needed to pass, though formats vary by state.

### Is the tanker endorsement hard?
It's one of the easier endorsements. Focus on liquid surge, baffles, and rollover prevention.

### Do I need a tanker endorsement to haul IBC totes?
If the totes are each over 119 gallons and total 1,000 gallons or more, and are loaded on the vehicle, the vehicle can meet the tank vehicle definition. Check with your carrier.
