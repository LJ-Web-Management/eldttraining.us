:::answer
**A Class A CDL lets you drive any combination of vehicles with a gross combination weight rating (GCWR) of 26,001 lbs or more, where the towed unit weighs more than 10,000 lbs** (49 CFR 383.91). That covers tractor-trailers, truck-and-trailer combinations, tankers, flatbeds, and livestock haulers. With the right endorsements, a Class A also covers most Class B and C vehicles. First-time Class A applicants must complete Class A ELDT theory and behind-the-wheel training before the skills test.
:::

## What can you drive with a Class A CDL?

- Tractor-trailers (semis, 18-wheelers)
- Truck and trailer combinations (for example, a dump truck pulling a heavy equipment trailer)
- Tanker trucks (with N or X endorsement)
- Flatbeds and car haulers
- Livestock trailers
- Doubles and triples (with T endorsement)
- Most Class B and C vehicles, with any required endorsements (P for buses, for example)

## Class A CDL requirements

- Age 18 (intrastate) or 21 (interstate)
- DOT medical certificate
- CLP with General Knowledge, Air Brakes, and **Combination Vehicles** tests passed
- 14-day CLP holding period
- **[Class A ELDT](/products/entry-level-driver-training-eldt-class-a)** theory (80%+) and BTW from registered providers
- Skills test in a representative combination vehicle

## What Class A ELDT covers

Class A theory follows Appendix A to 49 CFR Part 380 across five units: basic operation (including coupling and uncoupling), safe operating procedures, advanced operating practices, vehicle systems and malfunctions, and non-driving activities like hours of service and trip planning. Our Class A course packages this into 30 lessons (about 15 hours). BTW adds range skills (backing maneuvers, coupling) and public-road driving.

## How to get a Class A CDL

1. Get a DOT physical.
2. Pass the Class A knowledge tests for your CLP.
3. Start [Class A ELDT theory online](/products/entry-level-driver-training-eldt-class-a).
4. Hold your CLP 14 days while completing theory.
5. Complete Class A BTW with a registered provider.
6. Pass the skills test: pre-trip inspection, basic control, road test.

## Class A CDL jobs

Long-haul (OTR), regional, and local freight; dedicated routes; flatbed; reefer; tanker; hazmat; intermodal and port drayage; car hauling; and owner-operator work. See [CDL jobs by license](/guides/cdl-jobs).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a Class A CDL drive a Class B truck?
Yes. A Class A license lets you drive Class B and C vehicles too, as long as you hold any endorsements the vehicle requires (for example, P for a bus) and have no restriction that prevents it.

### How long does it take to get a Class A CDL?
Typically 4 to 8 weeks, with a 14-day minimum CLP period. Full-time Class A schools often run 3 to 7 weeks.

### How much does a Class A CDL cost?
Roughly $3,000 to $10,000 at private schools, much less with online ELDT theory plus separate BTW, or employer-paid training. Our Class A theory course is $129.99.

### What's the hardest part of getting a Class A?
Most people say the air brakes portion of the pre-trip inspection and the backing maneuvers. Theory training and lots of range practice help.
