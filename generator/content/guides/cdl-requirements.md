:::answer
**To get a CDL you must be at least 18 (21 for interstate driving or hazmat), hold a valid driver's license from your state of domicile, prove identity and lawful presence, pass a DOT physical, read and speak English sufficiently, not be prohibited in the FMCSA Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse, pass CDL knowledge tests for a learner's permit, complete ELDT for a first-time Class A or B, and pass the skills test.** Your state may add requirements such as residency documents or a clean driving-record period.
:::

## Federal CDL requirements checklist

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| Age | 18+ for intrastate; 21+ for interstate commerce (49 CFR 391.11) |
| Driver's license | Valid non-commercial license from your state of domicile |
| Identity and lawful presence | Documents proving U.S. citizenship or lawful permanent residence (rules for non-domiciled applicants are stricter) |
| Medical certification | DOT physical by a National Registry examiner, certificate valid up to 24 months |
| English proficiency | Read and speak English well enough to converse, understand signs, respond to officials, and complete reports |
| Clearinghouse | Not in "prohibited" status in the FMCSA Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse |
| Driving record | 10-year record check across all states; disqualifications must be resolved |
| Knowledge tests | General Knowledge plus class- and endorsement-specific tests for your CLP |
| CLP holding period | At least 14 days before the skills test |
| ELDT | Required for first-time Class A, Class B, B-to-A upgrade, and first-time H, P, S |
| Skills test | Vehicle inspection, basic control, and road test in a representative vehicle |

## Age requirements

You can get a CDL at 18 in most states, but it will be restricted to **intrastate** driving (within your state) until you turn 21. Interstate commerce, and in practice hazmat, require you to be 21. See [how old you have to be to get a CDL](/guides/how-old-to-get-a-cdl).

## Medical requirements

You'll need a DOT physical covering vision (at least 20/40 in each eye, with or without correction), hearing, blood pressure, and overall health, plus a urinalysis screen. Certain conditions (insulin-treated diabetes, vision in one eye, seizures) have specific rules or exemption programs. See [DOT physical and medical card](/guides/dot-physical-medical-card).

## English language proficiency

Federal rules (49 CFR 391.11(b)(2)) require commercial drivers to read and speak English sufficiently. Since June 25, 2025, inspectors have placed drivers out of service for English proficiency violations at roadside. See [CDL English language proficiency](/guides/cdl-english-language-proficiency).

## Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse

Since November 18, 2024, state licensing agencies must check the FMCSA Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse before issuing, renewing, upgrading, or transferring a CLP or CDL. A driver in "prohibited" status can't get or keep CDL privileges until completing the return-to-duty process.

## Class A vs Class B requirements

The personal requirements are the same. The differences are the tests and training:

- **Class A:** Combination Vehicles knowledge test, [Class A ELDT](/products/entry-level-driver-training-eldt-class-a), and a skills test in a combination vehicle.
- **Class B:** [Class B ELDT](/products/entry-level-driver-training-eldt-class-b) and a skills test in a single heavy vehicle.
- **Air brakes:** test in a vehicle with air brakes and pass the Air Brakes knowledge test, or your CDL will carry an air brake restriction.

## Endorsement requirements

- **Hazmat (H):** H knowledge test, [hazmat ELDT](/products/cdl-entry-level-driver-training-eldt-hazmat-h-endorsement), TSA security threat assessment. See [hazmat endorsement](/guides/hazmat-endorsement).
- **Passenger (P) and School Bus (S):** knowledge test, ELDT, and skills test in a representative vehicle.
- **Tanker (N) and Doubles/Triples (T):** knowledge tests only.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a high school diploma to get a CDL?
No federal education requirement exists. Some employers and schools set their own.

### Can I get a CDL with a DUI?
It depends on timing and your state. A DUI in a CMV, or certain offenses while holding a CDL, leads to disqualification periods. Older DUIs may affect employment more than eligibility. Check with your state licensing agency.

### Do I need a CDL to drive a box truck?
Only if the truck's GVWR is 26,001 lbs or more (Class B), it tows a heavy trailer (Class A), it carries 16+ passengers, or it hauls placarded hazmat. Many rental box trucks are under 26,001 lbs.

### Can non-citizens get a CDL?
Lawful permanent residents can apply like citizens. Rules for non-domiciled CDLs issued to certain foreign nationals have tightened and been subject to recent federal rulemaking and litigation, so check your state's current requirements.
