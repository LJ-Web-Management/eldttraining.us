:::answer
**A CDL permit, officially a commercial learner's permit (CLP), lets you drive a commercial vehicle on public roads while accompanied by a qualified CDL holder.** To get one, you need a valid driver's license, a DOT medical certificate, and passing scores on your state's CDL knowledge tests. You must hold the CLP for at least 14 days before taking the CDL skills test. A CLP is valid for up to one year (state rules vary). You don't need a CLP to start ELDT theory, but you need one for behind-the-wheel training on public roads.
:::

## CLP requirements

- Valid non-commercial driver's license from your state of domicile
- Minimum age (18 for intrastate, 21 for interstate)
- Proof of identity, lawful presence, and residency
- DOT medical examiner's certificate
- Passing scores on the required [knowledge tests](/guides/cdl-permit-test)
- Driving record check and Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse query

## Which knowledge tests you need

| Test | Who takes it |
|---|---|
| General Knowledge | Everyone |
| Air Brakes | Anyone testing in an air-brake vehicle (most trucks) |
| Combination Vehicles | Class A applicants |
| Passenger | P endorsement |
| School Bus | S endorsement (plus Passenger) |
| Tanker | N endorsement |
| Doubles/Triples | T endorsement |
| Hazmat | H endorsement (requires hazmat ELDT first) |

## CLP rules you must follow

- **Accompanied driving only.** A CLP holder must be accompanied by a CDL holder with the proper class and endorsements, seated in the front seat (or directly behind the driver on a bus where that's how the vehicle is designed).
- **14-day minimum.** You can't take the skills test until you've held the CLP at least 14 days.
- **Limited endorsements.** Only P, S, and N endorsements can be placed on a CLP. P and S CLP holders may not carry passengers other than trainers, examiners, and other trainees. N CLP holders may only drive empty tank vehicles, and may not drive a tank that previously held hazardous materials unless it has been purged of all residue.
- **No hazmat on a CLP.** You can't haul placarded hazardous materials with a CLP.

## How long is a CLP valid?

Federal rules allow a CLP to be valid for no more than one year from issuance. Some states issue shorter permits with a renewal option. If your CLP expires before you test, you may need to retake knowledge tests. Plan your ELDT and skills test within this window.

## CLP and ELDT: what order?

FMCSA doesn't require a CLP before ELDT theory. Many people complete online theory first because it covers the same material as the knowledge tests and can start immediately. You do need a CLP before behind-the-wheel training on public roads, and you need both theory and BTW reported to the Training Provider Registry before the skills test.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long do I have to wait after getting my CLP to take the CDL test?
At least 14 days under 49 CFR 383.25. Many drivers use that time to complete behind-the-wheel training.

### Can I drive alone with a CDL permit?
No. A qualified CDL holder must accompany you whenever you drive a CMV with a CLP.

### Do I need ELDT to get my CDL permit?
No. ELDT is required before the skills test (or the H knowledge test), not before the CLP.

### Can I get my CLP online?
Some states offer online knowledge testing or appointment scheduling, but most require you to visit an office for documents, photos, and vision screening. Check your state licensing agency's website.

### What happens if my CLP expires?
You may need to renew it or retake the knowledge tests, depending on your state. Your ELDT records stay in the registry.
