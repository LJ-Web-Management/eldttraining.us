## What behind-the-wheel training costs

Behind-the-wheel (BTW) training needs a truck or bus, fuel, insurance, a range, and an instructor, so it costs more than theory. Pricing varies widely by region and provider:

- **Hourly BTW** at independent schools is often priced per hour of truck time.
- **BTW packages** for Class A commonly run from roughly $1,500 to $5,000+ depending on how much practice you need.
- **Full CDL school programs** (theory + BTW + test prep) commonly range from about $3,000 to $10,000.
- **Company-sponsored training** may cost nothing upfront in exchange for a work commitment. See [paid CDL training](/guides/paid-cdl-training).

Because theory and BTW can come from different registered providers, many drivers save money by completing theory online first and then buying only the BTW they need.

## Why are some online ELDT courses $25?

You'll see ads for very cheap ELDT. Before buying, check:

- Is the provider currently listed on the [Training Provider Registry](/guides/training-provider-registry) for your training type? Training from a removed provider is invalid.
- Does the price include the final assessment and TPR reporting?
- Is there a real support phone number and refund policy?
- Is it the full curriculum for your license class, or an add-on module?

## Ways to save on ELDT

- **Do theory online** and shop BTW separately.
- **Ask your employer.** Many carriers, school districts, transit agencies, and municipalities reimburse ELDT.
- **Check workforce programs.** WIOA-funded programs and community colleges sometimes cover CDL training costs. See [free ELDT training](/guides/free-eldt-training).
- **Bundle endorsements** you'll need anyway (for example, P and S for school bus jobs) so you only take the skills test trip once.
- **Veterans** may be eligible for the military skills test waiver, which also exempts them from ELDT.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much is ELDT training online?
Online ELDT theory usually costs about $50 to $150. Our prices range from $49.99 (Tanker N) to $129.99 (Class A and X endorsement).

### Is ELDT included in CDL school tuition?
Usually. Most CDL schools registered on the TPR include both theory and BTW. If you already finished theory online with another registered provider, ask the school whether it offers a BTW-only price.

### Do I have to pay for ELDT again if I fail the CDL skills test?
No. Your ELDT record stays in the TPR. Failing the state skills test does not erase your training; you just retest according to your state's rules (and pay any retest fee).

### Can my employer pay for my ELDT course?
Yes. Employers can purchase courses for drivers. Contact us at (866) 429-6742 for help enrolling multiple drivers.

### Is the hazmat ELDT course cheaper than Class A?
Yes. Hazmat theory is shorter (about 5 hours) and costs $69.99 at ELDT Training. You'll also pay TSA for the security threat assessment.
