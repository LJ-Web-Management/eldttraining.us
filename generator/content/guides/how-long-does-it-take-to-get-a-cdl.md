:::answer
**Most people get a CDL in about 4 to 8 weeks.** The fastest possible path is a little over two weeks, because you must hold your commercial learner's permit for at least 14 days before the skills test. Full-time CDL school programs commonly run 3 to 7 weeks; part-time programs can take a few months. Doing ELDT theory online while you wait out the 14-day CLP period is the easiest way to save time.
:::

## Typical CDL timeline

| Step | Typical time |
|---|---|
| Book and pass DOT physical | 1 to 7 days |
| Study and pass CLP knowledge tests | 1 to 2 weeks |
| Mandatory CLP holding period | 14 days minimum |
| ELDT theory (online) | A few days to 2 weeks (overlaps the 14 days) |
| Behind-the-wheel training | A few days to 3 weeks |
| Schedule and take skills test | Days to several weeks, depending on test-site availability |
| **Total** | **About 4 to 8 weeks** |

## The fastest realistic path

1. **Week 1:** DOT physical, study the CDL manual, pass General Knowledge, Air Brakes, and Combination tests. Start [online ELDT theory](/products/entry-level-driver-training-eldt-class-a) the same day.
2. **Weeks 2 to 3:** Finish theory while your CLP ages 14 days. Book BTW training and a skills test date.
3. **Weeks 3 to 5:** Complete BTW, then take the skills test.

## What slows people down

- **Skills test appointment backlogs.** Some states have waits of several weeks. Book early; states can schedule your test before training is complete, they just can't administer it until ELDT is in the registry.
- **Failing a knowledge test.** Retest rules and waiting periods vary by state.
- **Medical issues** that require specialist paperwork (sleep apnea, diabetes, vision).
- **BTW availability** at popular schools.
- **Missing documents** for identity, residency, or lawful presence.

## How long is CDL school?

Full-time Class A programs often run 3 to 7 weeks; Class B programs are usually shorter. Part-time evening or weekend programs may take 2 to 4 months. Company-sponsored programs vary, often including a period of supervised driving with a trainer after you get your CDL. See [paid CDL training](/guides/paid-cdl-training).

## How long do endorsements add?

| Endorsement | Extra time |
|---|---|
| Hazmat (H) | ELDT about 5 hours online, plus TSA background check (often a few weeks) |
| Tanker (N) | Knowledge test only |
| Passenger (P) | ELDT about 6 hours online + BTW + skills test |
| School Bus (S) | ELDT about 5 hours online + BTW + skills test |
| Doubles/Triples (T) | Knowledge test only |

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get a CDL in 2 weeks?
Only barely. You must hold your CLP for 14 days before the skills test, so the absolute minimum is a little over two weeks from CLP to CDL, and that assumes ELDT and BTW are finished within that time and a test slot is available.

### How long does Class B take compared with Class A?
Class B is usually a bit faster because BTW doesn't include coupling or combination-vehicle maneuvers. The 14-day CLP rule still applies.

### How long does the CDL skills test take?
Usually 1.5 to 3 hours for all three parts: pre-trip inspection, basic control, and road test.

### Does ELDT add time to getting a CDL?
Not much if you do theory online during the 14-day CLP period. BTW was always part of learning to drive a truck; ELDT just requires that a registered instructor certify it.
