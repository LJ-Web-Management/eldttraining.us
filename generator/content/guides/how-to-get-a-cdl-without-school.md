:::answer
**Yes, you can get a CDL without attending a traditional CDL school, but you can't skip training.** Since February 7, 2022, first-time Class A and B applicants must complete ELDT from providers on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry. The most common "no school" path is: ELDT theory online, then behind-the-wheel training from an employer that's registered as a provider, a BTW-only provider, or a community program. Theory and BTW can come from different registered providers.
:::

## What changed in 2022

Before ELDT, you could study the manual, practice with any CDL holder, and test. Now your state must see ELDT theory **and** BTW records in the Training Provider Registry before your skills test. Practice with a friend or family member can still help, but it doesn't replace registered BTW training.

## Ways to get your CDL without a traditional school

### Online theory + BTW-only provider
Complete theory online (Class A $129.99 here), then buy BTW from a registered provider that sells range and road training separately. This is often the cheapest self-funded route.

### Online theory + employer BTW
Many carriers, construction companies, municipalities, and bus operators are registered BTW providers. Some will hire you with a CLP, train you, and pay for testing. Online theory you've already completed can make you more attractive to these employers.

### Company-sponsored programs
Some carriers run full programs at no upfront cost with a work commitment. See [paid CDL training](/guides/paid-cdl-training).

### Community colleges and workforce programs
Not exactly "no school," but often cheaper and sometimes grant-funded.

## Step-by-step

1. DOT physical and CLP knowledge tests.
2. [Online ELDT theory](/collections/all) for your class.
3. Find a TPR-registered BTW provider (search the [registry](/guides/training-provider-registry)).
4. Hold your CLP 14 days and complete BTW.
5. Take the skills test in a representative vehicle (your BTW provider may supply it).

## What you still can't skip

- Registered BTW training for Class A, B, P, and S
- The 14-day CLP holding period
- State knowledge and skills tests
- The DOT medical exam and Clearinghouse check

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get a CDL in Texas without school?
Yes, the same federal rules apply in every state: online ELDT theory from a registered provider plus registered BTW training. Texas DPS checks the TPR before the skills test. See [ELDT in Texas](/pages/state/tx).

### Can a family member with a CDL train me?
They can ride along while you practice with your CLP, but unless they're a qualified instructor with a TPR-registered provider, that practice doesn't count as ELDT BTW.

### Can I rent a truck for my skills test?
Some providers and test sites rent trucks. You'll still need registered BTW training before testing.

### Is online CDL training legitimate?
The theory portion, yes, when the provider is on the TPR. There's no legitimate "fully online CDL."
