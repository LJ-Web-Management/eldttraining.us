:::answer
**The CDL permit test is a set of multiple-choice knowledge tests at your state licensing agency.** Everyone takes General Knowledge (typically 50 questions). Most truck drivers also take Air Brakes (about 25), Class A applicants take Combination Vehicles (about 20), and endorsement tests are added as needed. You need about **80%** to pass each test. Pass them and you get your commercial learner's permit (CLP).
:::

## CDL knowledge tests at a glance

| Test | Typical questions | Who takes it |
|---|---|---|
| General Knowledge | 50 | Everyone |
| Air Brakes | 25 | Anyone driving air-brake vehicles |
| Combination Vehicles | 20 | Class A |
| Passenger | 20 | P endorsement |
| School Bus | 20 | S endorsement |
| Tanker | 20 | N endorsement |
| Doubles/Triples | 20 | T endorsement |
| Hazardous Materials | 30 | H endorsement (requires hazmat ELDT first) |

Question counts follow the common national format; your state may differ slightly. The passing score is typically 80% per test.

## What the General Knowledge test covers

- Vehicle inspection and the pre-trip routine
- Basic vehicle control: shifting, backing, steering
- Seeing, communicating, space and speed management
- Night driving, fog, winter, hot weather, mountain driving
- Railroad crossings and emergencies (skids, brake failure, fires)
- Hours of service and staying alert
- Alcohol, drugs, and illness
- Hazardous materials basics
- Cargo securement

## Study tips that work

1. **Read your state's CDL manual** sections for every test you'll take. Questions come from it.
2. **Take practice tests** until you consistently score 90%+.
3. **Learn the numbers:** following distances, air pressure ranges, stopping distances, placard thresholds.
4. **Do ELDT theory first.** Online theory covers the same knowledge areas in more depth and can start before your permit.
5. **Don't skip Air Brakes.** It's the test most people fail, and failing it puts an L restriction on your license.

## After you pass

You'll get your CLP. Hold it at least 14 days before the skills test, complete ELDT theory and BTW, and practice the [pre-trip inspection](/guides/pre-trip-inspection-checklist).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many questions are on the CDL permit test?
General Knowledge typically has 50 questions. Your total depends on which other tests you take: Air Brakes (about 25), Combination (about 20), and any endorsements.

### What score do you need to pass the CDL permit test?
Typically 80% on each test, for example 40 of 50 on General Knowledge.

### Can I take the CDL permit test online?
Some states offer online or remote knowledge testing; most require an in-person test. Check your state agency.

### Is the ELDT test the same as the permit test?
No. The ELDT theory assessment is given by your training provider and reported to the FMCSA registry. The permit tests are state knowledge tests. See [ELDT test](/guides/eldt-test).
