:::answer
**A Class B CDL lets you drive a single vehicle with a gross vehicle weight rating (GVWR) of 26,001 lbs or more, or such a vehicle towing a trailer of 10,000 lbs GVWR or less** (49 CFR 383.91). Think dump trucks, box trucks, cement mixers, garbage trucks, and city or school buses (with the P and S endorsements). First-time Class B applicants must complete Class B ELDT theory and behind-the-wheel training before the skills test.
:::

## What can you drive with a Class B CDL?

- Straight trucks and box trucks 26,001+ lbs GVWR
- Dump trucks and cement mixers
- Garbage and recycling trucks
- Delivery trucks for beverages, building supplies, and furniture
- Tow trucks (heavy-duty)
- City, transit, and tour buses (with P endorsement)
- School buses (with P and S endorsements)
- Class C vehicles, with required endorsements

You **can't** drive a tractor-trailer or tow a trailer over 10,000 lbs with a Class B.

## Class B CDL requirements

- Age 18 (intrastate) or 21 (interstate)
- DOT medical certificate
- CLP with General Knowledge (and Air Brakes, if your vehicle has air brakes)
- 14-day CLP holding period
- **[Class B ELDT](/products/entry-level-driver-training-eldt-class-b)** theory (80%+) and BTW from registered providers
- Skills test in a representative Class B vehicle

## Class B ELDT

Class B theory follows Appendix B to Part 380: basic operation, safe operating procedures, advanced operating practices, vehicle systems and malfunctions, and non-driving activities. It skips combination-vehicle topics like coupling. Our Class B course is 29 lessons, about 13 hours. Bus drivers also need [Passenger (P)](/products/cdl-entry-level-driver-training-eldt-passenger-p-endorsement) ELDT, and school bus drivers need [School Bus (S)](/products/cdl-entry-level-driver-training-eldt-school-bus-s-endorsement) ELDT too.

## Class B CDL jobs

Local delivery, construction, waste management, municipal public works, utilities, transit, school transportation, and charter or tour buses. Class B jobs are often local with home time every night.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a Class B CDL worth it?
For local work and bus driving, yes. If you might want tractor-trailer work later, consider Class A now: it covers Class B vehicles too, and upgrading later requires ELDT again.

### Do school bus drivers need a Class B CDL?
Most full-size school buses require a Class B with P and S endorsements. Smaller buses under 26,001 lbs may require Class C with P and S.

### Can I upgrade from Class B to Class A later?
Yes, but an upgrade requires Class A ELDT before the Class A skills test. See [Class B to Class A upgrade](/guides/class-b-to-class-a-upgrade).
