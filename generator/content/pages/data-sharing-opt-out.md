As described in our [Privacy Policy](/policies/privacy-policy), we collect personal information from your interactions with us and our website, including through cookies and similar technologies. We may also share this information with advertising partners so we can show you more relevant ads on other websites and measure our advertising.

Sharing personal information for targeted advertising based on your activity across different websites may be considered a "sale," "sharing," or "targeted advertising" under certain U.S. state privacy laws. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to opt out.

## How to opt out

- **Global Privacy Control:** If you visit with a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal enabled, we treat it as a request to opt out of targeted advertising for that browser and device.
- **Email or phone:** Email info@eldttraining.us with the subject "Opt out" or call (866) 429-6742.
- **Google ads:** Manage ad personalization in your Google account's ad settings.
- **Cookies:** Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.
