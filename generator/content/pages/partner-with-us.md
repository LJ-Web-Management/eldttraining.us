## Who we work with

- **CDL schools and BTW providers** that want to offer online theory alongside their range and road training
- **Motor carriers and private fleets** onboarding new drivers or adding endorsements
- **School districts and bus contractors** training Class B, Passenger, and School Bus drivers
- **Transit agencies and municipalities** training bus and public-works drivers
- **Workforce development programs** funding CDL training for job seekers

## What partners get

- All eight ELDT courses: Class A, Class B, B-to-A upgrade, Hazmat, Passenger, School Bus, Tanker, and X
- Completion reporting to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry for every driver
- Self-paced, mobile-friendly courses with audio narration
- Help enrolling groups of drivers and tracking completions
- Support by phone and email

## How it works

1. Tell us how many drivers you train and which courses you need.
2. We set up enrollment for your drivers.
3. Drivers complete theory online at their own pace.
4. We report each completion to the TPR, and your team handles behind-the-wheel training and testing.

Read our [ELDT guide for employers](/guides/eldt-for-employers) for compliance details.
